## Limits

- The “full Sep 2026 India AI intern skip list” was not in this repo. Named companies from the prompt were excluded. Anything not in that named list was judged from the live page.
- Wellfound blocks some non-browser clients. CerebralZip and Teal were read through a fetch that returned the job text on 29 Sep 2026.
- WaaS salary fields sometimes omit “/ monthly”. Those are SOFT even when the number looks like a stipend.
- Aggregators (Internshala mirrors, course blogs, LinkedIn-only hiring posts, referral boards) were not treated as official postings.
