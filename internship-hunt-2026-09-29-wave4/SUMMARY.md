## Limits

- Ashby has no public global search. Coverage is the 1,365 slugs above, not every Ashby customer.
- Greenhouse 404 means those eight companies are not on the public job-board token that matches their name. It does not prove they have zero jobs on some other host. Where another official host was found, it was read.
- NIT Uttarakhand’s 11 Sep 2026 C2S PDF returned 403, so that advertisement was not ranked from an older copy of the same project.
- Job-blog stipends (IIT Patna, IIT Hyderabad ML Engineer ranges on aggregator sites) were not treated as official.
