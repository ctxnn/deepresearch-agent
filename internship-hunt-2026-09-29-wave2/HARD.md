# HARD — internships with an official stipend at or above ₹20,000/month

Checked 29 Sep 2026. Research only. Nothing here was applied to.

HARD means all of the following were true on the live posting:

- It is an internship (not a full-time new-grad role).
- The posting states pay. No stipend was estimated, converted into a rupee figure the company did not write, or copied from a different role.
- That stated pay is at or above ₹20,000/month. A posting in USD is included only when the stated amount is unambiguously above that floor. The USD figure below is the posting's figure.
- The company is not on the wave-2 skip list in `SKIPPED.md`.
- The source is YC Work at a Startup, Ashby, Greenhouse, Lever, or Wellfound.
- Location is India (remote or Bangalore preferred) or the posting explicitly offers visa sponsorship.
